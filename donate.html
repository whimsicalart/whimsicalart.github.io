Donate to WhimsicalArt

WhimsicalArt is free, open source, and will stay that way. If the app has been useful to you and you want to say thanks, a voluntary donation helps keep development going.

Important — read before you donate

Donations are entirely voluntary. You are not expected to donate, and there is no requirement to do so — the app is complete and usable without giving us anything.

Donating does not buy you any feature, service, benefit, priority support, or anything else in exchange. It is a gift of appreciation only. The app offers the same features to everyone, regardless of whether or how much anyone gives.

Ways to donate

We are still setting up our donation accounts across platforms. Check back soon.

What donations go toward

  • Development time and maintaining the project
  • Infrastructure and app store distribution (Google Play, F-Droid)
  • Preparing the releases for public stores
  • Realistically: good coffee

Prefer not to donate?

That’s completely fine. You can still support the project by starring the repository on GitHub, reporting bugs, translating, improving documentation, or contributing code.
