Beautiful photo editing, without the catch.

WhimsicalArt is a modern Android photo editor built on current APIs and best practices — with the classic editing tools you love and none of the ads, paywalls, or account requirements that ruined the rest.

Everything you’d expect. Nothing you won’t.

A full editing suite that runs entirely on your device. Every edit is a reorderable, reversible layer.

Classic Filters

Color grades, style tone-maps, and lens looks with intensity control and multi-select.

Full Toolset

Crop, rotate, and flip — plus brightness, contrast, saturation, exposure, shadows, highlights, temperature, tint, vignette, and sharpening.

Beauty Tools

On-device skin smoothing, blemish removal, face reshaping, and makeup. Fully offline.

Text & OCR

Editable text overlays with fonts, styles, shadows, and strokes — plus recognize text straight from your photos.

Stickers & Decorations

Stickers, emoji, mosaic, blur brush, magic pen, and frames. Drag, scale, reorder, and refine.

Collage Maker

Combine multiple photos into beautiful, customizable grid and free-form layouts.

Background Toolbox

Object removal with content-aware fill, background blur, clone stamp, healing brush, and background replace.

Save & Share

JPEG/PNG export at your chosen quality and resolution, straight into your gallery or the share sheet.

Layers

Every edit stays independent and reversible. Reorder the stack or merge layers to flatten.
